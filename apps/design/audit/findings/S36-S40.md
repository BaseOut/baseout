## 2 First five seconds

**These are the only screens a logged-out person sees, so this lens carries the most weight here.**

- **`/login`** (`audit/shots/S36-S38-auth-390.png`). Eye lands on the wordmark, then "Welcome
  back", then a soft-primary **Continue with Airtable**, then the sentence that earns the click —
  *"Uses your Airtable account email — doesn't connect your data."* That is the right order, and
  that sentence is the best copy on the front door. *Where am I* — answered by the wordmark and
  the brand panel at ≥1024. *What do I do next* — answered twice, ranked, which is the point of
  `login-methods` Decision 1.
- **`/register`** — identical composition, correct strings flipped ("Create your account", "…in
  setup", "Already have an account? Sign in"). Nothing to separate a reader's mental model.
- **`/welcome?fixture=trial`** — the eye lands on **"Welcome, reese@baseout.design"**, a raw
  email set in a 24px heading (F20), on the only screen in the family with **no logo and no
  footer** (F9). The form itself is short, honest about its length, and has no progress bar.
- **`/404`** (`audit/shots/S39-404-generic-vs-scoped.png`) — the eye lands on an **80px** compass
  glyph, the largest single element on any surface in this batch, above a 24px `h3` and a 16px
  sentence. It is the only page in the app that answers *where am I* with an illustration.
- **`/styleguide`** at 1440 — nav, breadcrumb, entry title, provenance tag, summary, decision
  tables, Do/Don't, live preview, code. Genuinely good. At 390 the eye lands on a full-width
  sidebar and a **126px** ribbon of one-word lines (F4, `audit/shots/S40-styleguide-390-768.png`).

## 3 Navigation and orientation

- **Arrival.** `/login` ⇄ `/register` cross-link in the footer; `/welcome` is reached from a link
  in an email and links nowhere. The association fork exits to `/login` ("Use a different email")
  and `/welcome` — both correct.
- **Departure from `/welcome` is a one-way door with no back link and no logo to click.**
- **The URL does not carry the sent state.** In production `sentHref` is absent by design
  (`lib/auth/types.ts:41-45`), so the panel swap is `classList` only (`LoginView.astro:205-206`)
  with no `history` write: not linkable, and a refresh returns to the form (F10). Compare
  `components/ui/sectionTabs.ts`, which `history.replaceState`s `?tab=` for exactly this reason —
  the rule the catalog states in `pattern-section-tabs`, and the same defect J01-F8 filed against
  the wizard.
- **The 404 marks nothing active.** Measured: `[aria-current]` count **0** on `/404` and on
  `/no-such-page-xyz`. Correct — there is no section to mark — but it is the same null J01-F25
  filed against the wizard, so it is a shell-wide behaviour, not a 404 decision.
- **No breadcrumb renders**, although `404.astro:7` and `[...slug].astro:7` both construct
  `[Home, 404]`. J01-F28 owns this; not re-filed.
- **`/styleguide` writes `#<entry-id>` with `replaceState` and listens to `hashchange`**
  (`styleguide.astro` inline script) — the position is addressable and deep-linkable. Better than
  several product surfaces.
- **Page titles disagree three ways** (F17): `Sign In — Baseout` · `Page Not Found | Baseout` ·
  bare `Welcome`.

## 7 Cross-surface consistency

Against the reference surfaces (Schema · Data · Reports):

- **Scroll model.** Schema, Data and Reports scroll inside `#layout-content`; the **auth shell
  cannot scroll at all** (`auth.css:6`, `h-screen overflow-hidden`) — the only unscrollable
  surface in the product (F1). D22 is named "one scroll model, one narrow form"; this is outside it.
- **The 390 floor.** Every shell route carries `#too-narrow`; the three signed-out routes do not
  (F3), which is also why F1 has never been seen.
- **The type ladder.** Schema/Data/Reports step every rung at 1280. The 404 steps **none** — 80 ·
  24 · 16 identical at 390 and 1440 (F7) — and `fieldset-label` prints 12/14/16 depending on its
  parent, app-wide (F5).
- **Heading level.** Every product view opens on an `h1`/`h2` page title; the auth family has no
  `h1` at all and the path 404 opens on an `h3` (F8).
- **Addressable state.** `sectionTabs.ts` writes `?tab=`; the login panel swap writes nothing
  (F10) — the same divergence as J01-F8.
- **Empty-state anatomy.** `pattern-empty-state` is honoured exactly by the scoped 404 and
  contradicted by the path 404 **inside the same file** (F6).
- **Theme.** Every product surface follows the user's theme; the auth routes are hard-locked dark
  (F12).
- **Date/number formatting** — nothing on these five surfaces formats a date or a count except
  `© {new Date().getFullYear()}`. No divergence to report.

## 8 Copy and tone

Second person throughout, no exclamation marks, no "Awesome!", verbs on buttons. This is the
best-written family in the app — and the defects are the two places it stops being one voice.

**Right, and worth keeping:** *"Uses your Airtable account email — doesn't connect your data. You
choose which bases Baseout can read later."* · *"We email you a link — no password to remember or
reset."* · *"If an account exists for … we sent a sign-in link. The link expires in 5 minutes."*
(privacy-preserving, and `specs/03-login.md:74-77` asks that it never change) · *"You closed the
Airtable window, so nothing was signed in. Pick a way to continue."* · *"Trust lasts 30 days,
then expires on its own — nothing to remember to turn off."* · *"Lost your phone and your backup
codes? Ask an admin in your organization to reset two-factor for you."*

**Defects:**
- The lockout screen states its message **twice, 4px apart, at 14px and 16px** (F16).
- *"Didn't receive it? **Try a different email**"* answers a question nobody asked; the user's
  need is *send it again* (F10).
- *"Welcome, reese@baseout.design"* greets a person by the identifier the form exists to
  replace (F20).
- The 404's *"The page you're looking for doesn't exist or has been moved"* never says **which**
  page, and offers no second exit — `specs/15-not-found.md:60-64` asks for 1–2 (F19).
- `/register` says *"Start protecting your data in minutes"* where `specs/04-register.md:48` says
  *"Start backing up your Airtable in minutes"* — the shipped line is better; the spec is stale
  (F18).

## 9 Foreign or surprising

- **A form that ignores you.** Pressing the app's front-door primary with an empty field produces
  no reaction of any kind (F2). Nothing else in the product does that.
- **A page that cannot scroll.** Rotating a phone on `/welcome` deletes the submit button, both
  ends of the card, with no scrollbar (F1). This is the single most surprising behaviour found.
- **A typo that is confirmed.** `not-an-email` produces "we sent a sign-in link" and a five-minute
  wait (F2).
- **A dark door into a light house** (F12).
- **A 404 that is 30% illustration** — an 80px glyph on a utility admin tool whose principles say
  *avoid hero illustrations* (`specs/00-design-principles.md:35-36`); the same file's other branch
  uses a 48px tile (F6).
- **The catalog cannot be searched.** 112 of 113 entries are `hidden`; find-in-page over the rules
  returns nothing (F15).
- **The catalog breaks its own floor in its own chrome** — a 9.5px "Default" pill sitting beside
  the rule that forbids ~10px (F14).

## 10 Density and rhythm

- **The auth column is well-tuned at every width that fits**: card `max-width: 360px`, 16px gutter
  below 1024 and 40px above, 4px `.auth-form` gaps, 8px note offsets, all on the 4px grid; the
  50/50 split is argued in-file against the 480px rail that was tried first (`auth.css:93-95`).
  Measured card widths: 360 @1440 · 358 @390 · 343 @375. `documentElement.scrollWidth −
  clientWidth = 0` at every width tested — **0 horizontal overflow across all five surfaces**,
  which is the number `specs/16-responsive.md:213-218` says must stay 0.
- **The one rhythm break is vertical, not horizontal** (F1).
- **The 2FA screen has three caption sizes in 120px** — 16px hint, 14px trust label, 12px note
  (F5, F16).
- **The 404 is the loudest page in the app**: 80 / 24 / 16 against a product whose narrow body
  copy is 12–14 (F7).
- **`/styleguide` at 1440 is dense and even**; below 1024 it is not a density problem but a
  content-destruction one (F4).

### Harness limitations and NOT-OURS (recorded, **not** product findings)

- **`?fixture=trial` is a no-op on `/welcome`.** `resolveWelcome` (`apps/design/src/fixtures/auth.ts:141-148`)
  reads only `?state=`; the email comes from `Astro.locals.user`. The worklist URL and the declared
  smoke variant `'/welcome': ['fixture=trial']` (`smoke.mjs:245`) therefore walk the same render as
  the bare route. **HARNESS.**
- **`smoke.mjs` declares no variants for `/login` or `/register`**, so ten guarded branches
  (`?state=sent|sso-error|challenge|wrong-code|lockout|backup-code|trusted-device|assoc|assoc-pending`
  plus `&entry=sso`) are requested by nothing. Each is a different render behind a `switch` — exactly
  what the VARIANTS table exists for. **HARNESS**, and the cheapest coverage win in the file.
- **The scoped-404 props are passed only from `apps/design` route shells** (`sources/detail.astro`,
  `destinations/detail.astro`, `reports/[id].astro`, `reports/run/[runId].astro`). Whether the real
  backend resolves a missing id to this component is **NOT-OURS**.
- **Whether a signed-out request ever reaches `/404`** — both shells render `SidebarLayout`
  unconditionally, i.e. a full app chrome with a Space switcher. `specs/15-not-found.md:52-54` asks
  for the shell on purpose. For a signed-out visitor this would be wrong, but session routing is
  **NOT-OURS**.
- **No 500 / 403 surface exists.** `specs/15-not-found.md:75-79` states this and calls it separate
  work. Recorded, not filed.
- **Browser-tab race.** Four scouts shared the MCP's single global selected page; two navigations
  landed on another agent's tab and were restored immediately (page 30 → `/settings?tab=billing`).
  All geometry above was taken in iframes for this reason. Both tabs this scout opened were closed.

## What is good here — do not touch

- **The two-factor screen is the best-reasoned surface in this batch.** The backup-code swap is
  reachable **during a lockout** (`AuthChallengeView.astro:127-130` sits outside the disabled
  form), "trust this device" states its bound out loud and says it expires on its own, the
  challenge is its own route so it survives a refresh, and the lost-phone footer names a real
  human path instead of a dead end. The reasons are written in the file header (`:9-13`). Keep all
  of it.
- **The association fork.** Two equal-weight `btn-secondary` cards with **deliberately no
  `btn-primary`** (`:8-10`), a screen that is identical from both entry paths, and a request that
  never blocks — the banner, the badge and *"Continue setting up your own account"* all appear in
  the same tick (`:125-135`). This is the pattern J01-F9 was asking the wizard for, already built.
- **The ranked two-path sign-in.** Airtable above, magic link below, one solid primary between
  them, **no method tabs** — and the note under the SSO button that separates "sign in with
  Airtable" from "let Baseout read my bases". That sentence is the highest-value copy on the
  front door.
- **The privacy-preserving sent copy** and the up-front 5-minute expiry.
- **The bfcache handler** (`LoginView.astro:186-191`) — the commonest way back from Airtable's
  consent screen restores the page with a dead spinning primary, and this is the only place in the
  app that noticed. The reasoning is in the comment; do not "simplify" it away.
- **Register is Login in `register` mode** (`RegisterView.astro:1-18`) — one implementation, two
  screens, with the reason written down. Exactly the variance reduction this audit exists to find.
- **The `?state=` fixture vocabulary** — ten auth states addressable by URL and documented in the
  fixture header. Nothing else in the app documents its own state space this well.
- **`#too-narrow` itself.** Measured exact at the boundary: `none` at 390, `flex` at 375. The gate
  is right; it is only in the wrong place (F3).
- **The scoped 404** — its props, their doc comments, and the catalog entry that governs them. It
  is the correct half of F6 and must be the model, not the casualty.
- **The catalog's decision-table format** — Use / When / Why with the Default row marked, the
  Do/Don't columns, the provenance tag, the live preview above the copyable code, and the deep-link
  hash. At ≥1024 this is a genuinely strong reference document, and F4/F14/F15 are about the frame
  around it, never about that.
- **Zero horizontal overflow on all five surfaces at every width tested** (375 · 390 · 768 · 1024 ·
  1440). That number is the thing `specs/16-responsive.md:213-218` says must stay 0, and it is 0.
