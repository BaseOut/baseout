## 1 Job

- **X04.** The user's job at an empty state is to find out *whether the product is broken or merely
  new*, and to get to the one place that fills it. `specs/00-design-principles.md` — trust signals
  first: *is it working · what's next*. An empty state is the trust signal at its weakest moment.
- **X05.** The user's job at an alert is to know *how alarmed to be* and *what to do*. The catalog
  states the intent explicitly (`storybook.ts:1644` `alert`): "Colour carries the meaning."
- **X08.** The user's job at a status word is to read one column down a list and know which rows need
  them. `specs/` states the density target (Linear / Vercel / Plaid) but **does not anywhere define
  the app's state vocabulary** — there is no master state-word list in `specs/`. **UNSTATED, and that
  is itself the root finding for X08:** D19 was written to create that table and it does not yet exist.

## 2 First five seconds

Not a single screen, so the lens equivalent: *does a user who has learned one empty state / one alert
/ one badge recognise the next one?*

- **X04 — no.** 28 distinct empty-state class families across 30 files (census below). Two of the 28
  are shared via `global.css` (`.cl-empty`, `.pk-empty`); the other 26 are file-local scoped CSS.
- **X05 — mostly yes, and this is the healthiest of the three lenses.** 162 uses of `alert-*`, of
  which 149 carry `alert-soft`. The four-colour table is broadly obeyed. The failures are at the
  edges: 6 live solid alerts, 4 hand-rolled tinted boxes that are alerts wearing another name, a
  fifth colour (`primary`) that the catalog does not define, and one glyph carrying two severities.
- **X08 — no.** The catalog names `components/ui/Badge.astro` as the reference "the app-wide status
  pill … the pattern every view should converge on", and it is imported by **6 files / 10 call
  sites**, against **175** raw `badge-soft` strings and **68** raw `badge-ghost` strings. The
  declared reference is used by ~5% of the badges in the app.

## 3 Navigation and orientation

An empty state is a navigational object: it is the only chrome left on a degraded page. The
`pattern-empty-state` "one real exit" rule is where this lens meets navigation, and the census below
scores each family on whether it has one. Nothing in X05/X08 is navigational except D19 item 1
(a verb rendered as a badge is a control the user cannot press) — already filed as J03-F8.

### X08 — status vocabulary

The catalog's declared reference `Badge.astro` is imported by 6 files (`IntegrationsView` — dead,
`SecurityPanel`, `SourcesView`, `SourceDetailView`, `DestinationsView`, `DestinationDetailView`) for
10 call sites. Two of its ten variants have **zero callers outside the component's own map**:
`secondary` → `badge-secondary` (1 occurrence app-wide = the map line), `tertiary` → `badge-accent`
(likewise, `Badge.astro:24-25`). Its `default` → `badge-ghost` variant gained its first callers on
2026-08-13 (`6e8ead0`), and they are the two `Unknown` fallbacks — everything else that wants neutral
writes `badge-ghost` directly, 68 times.

**The word census.** 28 status→colour registries live in `apps/web/src`. Across them the app uses
**12 distinct words for "it succeeded"** — `Succeeded` · `Backed up` · `Done` · `Resolved` · `Healthy` ·
`OK` · `No issues` · `Green` · `Valid` · `Trial run` · `Trial complete` · `succeeded (trial)` — **10 for
"it failed"** — `Failed` · `Failure` · `Failed to generate` · `Auth failed` · `Broken` · `Disconnected` ·
`Lost access` · `Red` · `Needs attention` · `Invalid` — and **5 for in-progress** plus 2 for queued
(`Running` · `Generating` · `Connecting` · `Refreshing tokens` · `Cancelling`; `Queued` · `Pending`).
The same word also changes colour across surfaces:

**Colour semantics as declared vs as used.** `SpaceHomeView.astro:136-144` is the only registry in the
app that maps every level of a state machine to a tone *and* handles `unknown`:

```
broken:'paused'  running:'running'  failed:'failed'
degraded:'warn'  overdue:'warn'  ok:'ok'  unknown:'idle'
```

7 levels → 6 tone names → **5 distinct paints** (`is-paused` and `is-warn` are the same
`--color-warning` at 8%, `:653` and `:658`). `unknown` → `is-idle` gets a real neutral paint
(`:659, :673-674, :681`) — the app **does** follow the "a registry that meets an unknown state must
not guess" ruling here, and this is the implementation to converge on. The defect is the *name*:
`broken` is painted through a class called `is-paused`, so the app's most severe connection state
reads, in source, as the calmest one — and the next person to touch `.hm-status.is-paused` will be
styling two unrelated states at once.

## 8 Copy and tone

The `pattern-empty-state` entry bans `"Nothing here!"`-shaped titles. Live counter-examples:
`No documents yet` (`SchemaDocs.astro:98`, already S15-F10), `No backups yet`
(`BackupsListView.astro:289`), `No Airtable source yet` (`IntegrationsSetupWizard.astro:206`). All
three name the *absence* rather than what will appear and when — the reference title does the
opposite (`Data appears after your first backup`, `DataView.astro:251`). Sentence bodies are good
throughout; the titles are where the register did not sweep.

The one live failure-copy deck speaks in a developer's register:
`StoragePicker.astro:478-492` — *"Your browser dropped the OAuth handoff cookie during the
round-trip"*, *"The OAuth state parameter didn't match what we sent"* — and prints
`error_code: <code>` to the user at `:500`. The dead deck for the same eight codes speaks in the
user's: *"Your session expired before Airtable returned. Please try again."*
(`IntegrationsView.astro:50-60`).

## 10 Density and rhythm

Empty-state paddings: `2rem` (`.sch-empty`), `3rem 1.5rem` (`.bl-empty`), `2rem` (`.bst-empty`),
`.75rem` (`.au-nomatch`), `24px 4px` (`.rs-nomatch`), `8px` (`.tbf-nomatch`, `.ff-nomatch`),
`16px` (`.pk-empty`). `3rem 1.5rem` = 48/24 and `24px 4px` — the `4px` is on-grid but reads as a
different rhythm from every sibling. **No off-grid values found**; the 4px-grid rule holds across
this lens. `.rpd-empty-ic` at `2.75rem` = 44px is the one tile off the 4px grid's usual 48.

  refuses in its "Not this pattern" clause for filter-no-match.
