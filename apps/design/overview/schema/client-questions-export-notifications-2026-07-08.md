Schema — a few questions for you

Q1. Export: what should it give you?
Two kinds are possible: an image / PDF of the diagram or report (for sharing), and a data file (CSV / JSON) of the schema (for spreadsheets or audits). Do you want both, or just one per tab? Should Browse get a CSV export too (a plain list of all bases/tables/fields)?

Q2. Export: full schema, or just what's on screen?
Every tab has filters. When you export, should it output your current filtered view, or always the whole schema? Our lean: the current view by default, with a clear "Export everything" option, and one consistent export panel on every tab (pick format, pick scope, go).

Q3. Notifications: turn the top-bar bell into one notification center?
The bell already exists but opens nothing today. Proposal: make it the single home for all alerts. Examples: schema changed (with breaking-change flags), health score dropped, connection needs reconnect, backup finished or failed, an automation turned off / interface unpublished, a chat answer saved as a doc. Each links straight to the right spot. Does this direction work for you?

Q4. Notifications: one place to control them?
A single settings page: choose what you're notified about and how (in-app, email, or a daily/weekly digest) and at what level (whole Space, per base, or per watched table). Plus one simple "watch" toggle on a base/table/automation, instead of separate subscriber lists in each tab. Which alerts matter most for v1?
