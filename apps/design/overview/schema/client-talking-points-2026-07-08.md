# Schema — a few open calls for you

_Most of the smaller gaps we'll just close on our side. These are the ones where we need your
steer — the product decisions only you can make._

1. **Docs — what happens when you create a new document?**
   Blank page, a few ready-made templates, or an AI-generated first draft? And should docs be
   organized (folders, sharing), or stay a simple private notebook?

2. **Export — what should it actually give you?**
   The button is there but the choices aren't set yet. An image of the diagram, or the data as
   CSV/JSON? And just what's on screen (current filters), or the whole schema?

3. **Notifications — do you want them, and where?**
   Right now nothing alerts you. Should users get pinged when the schema changes, or when a base's
   health drops — in-app, email, a digest? This is the one bigger piece that's completely unspecced.

4. **Settings — what belongs there?**
   A few controls point into Settings (health-scoring rules, credits/billing). The main call: can
   users tune how the health score is calculated, or do we keep that fixed?

5. **Health — read-only, or actionable?**
   Is Health just advice, or should users act on it — dismiss/snooze an issue, turn a metric off?
   That decides how much we build there.

6. **The "Ask about your schema" shortcut** — a full mini-chat you can type into, or just a button
   that opens the main Chat tab?
