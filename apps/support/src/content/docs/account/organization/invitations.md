---
title: Invitations
description: The two ways somebody ends up in your organization, and why one of them arrives without you doing anything.
---

People get into an organization from two directions, and only one of them starts with you.

## They ask

Somebody signing up with an email address at your domain is offered the choice of requesting to join
your organization instead of creating their own. That request comes to an admin, who approves or
declines it. See [Joining an organization](/account/organization/joining/).

This is the path that happens whether or not anybody planned for it, which is worth knowing if you
expected to control who is in the organization. What controls it is the approval, not the request.

## You ask

The other direction is an invitation you send to an address, which is the ordinary way to bring in
somebody whose email is at a different domain, or who has not signed up yet.

## An invitation is not access to your data

Worth being exact about, because it is the thing people worry about and the answer is reassuring in
a way that is easy to miss.

:::note
Joining an organization gives somebody the organization's Spaces and history. It does not give them
anything in Airtable, Notion or ClickUp: a Source carries the access of the person who authorized
it, and that person is not the new member. Nor does it reach into your Destination, which
authenticates against your storage under its own credentials.
:::

So the blast radius of an invitation is the Baseout side of things, and the platform side is
unchanged. See [Sources](/connections/sources/).

## What happens to the invited person's own account

An email address is an account, so somebody who already has their own Baseout account and their own
organization is a person with somewhere to be. Joining is a choice they make, not something done to
them, which is the same principle as the signup fork.

## What is not settled yet

The invitation screen, whether an invitation carries a role, how long one stays open, and what
happens to an unclaimed one are not decided. This page will describe the flow once there is a flow
to describe rather than a plausible one made up here.
