Most of this manual is the same whoever you are. Backing something up, getting it back, being
billed: none of that changes with the platform on the other end of the connection. A handful of
questions do change, and they are the ones people write in about.

## One page per platform, inside the chapter it belongs to

There are now thirty platform pages, ten for each of Airtable, ClickUp and Notion: what we back up,
how long a run takes, what happens to deleted and archived items, how the connection is made, what
that connection can see, how field and property types map, attachments, comments, identifiers, and
restoring.

They are not filed on a shelf of their own. Each one sits in the chapter whose question it answers,
so "What we back up" is inside Backing up with three rows under it, and "Connecting" is inside
Sources and destinations with three rows under it. The manual is sorted by what you are trying to
do, and a shelf sorted by product would have been the one place you had to change gear.

## Narrow the manual to your platform

The chips above the sidebar filter the whole tree. Pick Notion and every chapter keeps its shape
while each of those three-row groups keeps one row: the same reading order, with the two answers
that are not yours out of the way.

Pages that do not name a platform are never filtered out. Billing, sign-in, your organization and
the rest of your account are identical whoever you back up, so a filter that hid them would be
answering a question nobody asked. The filter survives navigation, and a page you reach while a
filter is on says which platform it belongs to before its first sentence.

## Differences at a glance

When the question is comparative rather than specific, [Platform differences at a
glance](/reference/platform-differences/) puts the three side by side in one table: what each
platform's API exposes, what it does not, and where that leaves a backup. It is the page to read
before assuming something works the same way twice.
