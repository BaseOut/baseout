Writing to us used to end at a submit button. You got a confirmation screen, the reply arrived by
email, and the portal had no memory of any of it.

## Two forms became one door

There were two addresses for starting a conversation. Both now lead to [one contact
page](/contact/), and the old URLs redirect rather than 404, so a link somebody saved or pasted into
a message still lands somewhere useful.

The page asks what kind of message this is first, and the form changes to suit: reporting something
broken asks for different things than asking about an invoice does. The door itself stays neutral,
because most of the people standing in front of it are not reporting a problem.

## My requests, in the header

[My requests](/requests/) lists every case opened from your email address, split into open and
closed, with the reference and the state of each one. It is a header destination rather than a line
at the foot of the contact page, because opening a case and reading the ones you opened are
different jobs at different frequencies.

## A case is a thread, not a submission

Open one and you see the whole exchange in order: what you wrote, what came back, and anything
attached along the way. Replies made by email appear in the same thread, so there is one record of
the conversation rather than two halves of it in different places.

The chat in the documentation answers immediately and never becomes a case on its own. When it runs
out of answers it hands you to the contact page with what you have already said carried across, so
the first thing you write to a person is not the thing you just finished explaining.
