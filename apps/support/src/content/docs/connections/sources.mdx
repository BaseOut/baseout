A **Source** is a connection to the platform you are backing up. It belongs to your account rather
than to one Space, so you add it once and reuse it wherever you need it. The Sources list shows each
one's name, how it was authorized, its status, how many Spaces use it, and how much it can see.

What a Source *is* does not change with the platform. How you authorize one does, and so does what
that authorization can reach.

## Which method to use

Both produce a working Source. The choice is not about Baseout, it is about who the grant belongs to
and how it is changed afterwards, so it is answered on the platform's own page:
[Connecting Airtable](/platforms/airtable/connecting/),
[Connecting ClickUp](/platforms/clickup/connecting/),
[Connecting Notion](/platforms/notion/connecting/).

## Why one Source sees more than another

A Source sees exactly what its authorization covers, and the person who made the grant is what sets
that. Two Sources on the same platform, authorized by two people, will see different things. What
was left out is not hidden from you inside Baseout, it is invisible to Baseout, which cannot know it
exists. That is the usual reason something is missing from a picker: see
[My bases are missing from the picker](/troubleshooting/missing-bases/).

New objects are found by re-checking, never announced. No platform tells Baseout that a base was
created, so the Source looks again and reports what it found. New objects are surfaced for you to
add rather than added for you, because nothing should enter a backup, or a bill, without you
choosing it.

## More than one account

An account can hold several Sources, including more than one on the same platform, told apart by
their name and the account that authorized them. A Space uses **exactly one**, and different Spaces
can use different Sources.

## Removing one, and moving a Space

Removal is guarded while Spaces still use it: unlink them first, and the warning states how many are
affected.

Moving a Space onto a different Source is a deliberate edit, and it resets that Space's base
selection. Bases belong to a Source, so the ones you had chosen do not exist on the new one. You are
asked to confirm before the selection is cleared, and declining leaves both the Source and the
selection as they were.

## Reconnecting

Status, reconnect and the re-check for new objects all live on the Source rather than inside a
Space, so one fix serves every Space using it. See
[Reconnecting a broken connection](/connections/reconnecting/).
