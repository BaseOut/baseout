title: Data overview
description: Browse, trace and search the records inside your backups. Read-only.

The Data section is the contents of your backups, as opposed to Schema, which is their structure.
It is read-only: nothing you do here writes back to Airtable.

Its tabs are grouped as **Data Elements** (Records, Attachments, Comments), **Between backups**
(Changelog) and **Knowledge** (Docs, Chat). Everything you see is as of a particular backup, and the
page states which one at the top.
