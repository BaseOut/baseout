---
title: Presets and export
description: Saving a view of your data, and getting it out.
---

A *preset* is a saved combination of base, table, filters and visible fields. Once you save a
preset its base and table are locked — to work on a different table you create a new preset or
duplicate the current one, which arrives as an unsaved draft. Unsaved edits survive a reload as a
draft rather than disappearing.

Export writes the current view out as CSV or JSON, scoped either to the open tab or to all open
tabs, with an Excel-compatible option for accents and emoji.
