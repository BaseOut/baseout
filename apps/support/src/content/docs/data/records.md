The Records tab shows one table at a time from one base, as a grid. Clicking a row opens the record
in a panel, where you can see its fields, its linked records, and its history across backups.

This page will cover moving between bases and tables, what the record panel holds, and how to see a
record as it stood at an earlier backup.

## Questions this page will answer

- How do I get to a specific table?
- How do I find a record when I only know one field's value?
- What does "view as of" show me?
- I clicked a linked record and nothing happened — why?
- Why does the row count say "approximately"?
