## Is Baseout related to On2Air?

Yes — Baseout is the next generation of On2Air Backups, from the same team.

## Does Baseout ever change my Airtable data?

Only when you explicitly restore, and even then it writes into new tables rather than over the
originals. Backups, Schema and Data are all read-only.
