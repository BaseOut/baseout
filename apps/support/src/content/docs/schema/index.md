The Schema section shows the structure Baseout captured: bases, tables, fields, views, and how they
link. It is read-only, and it has nothing to show until the first backup has run — everything on it
is reconstructed from a snapshot, not read live from Airtable.

Its tabs sit in four groups: **Explore** (Browse, Visualize, Relationships), **App layer**
(Automations, Interfaces), **Monitor** (Changelog, Health) and **Knowledge** (Docs, Chat). The pages
below follow those groups.
