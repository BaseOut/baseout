---
title: Visualize and Relationships
description: The ER diagram, and the map of how your tables link.
---

**Visualize** draws your Space's structure as a read-only ER diagram — tables as nodes, linked-record
fields as edges — and clicking a node opens the same detail panel Browse uses.

**Relationships** is the same information as a per-base table: linked records, formulas, rollups,
lookups, last-modified fields and synced views. Some of those Baseout can only infer, and inferred
ones carry a Confirm/Dismiss decision so you can tell them apart from the certain ones.

## Questions this page will answer

- What do the arrows in the diagram mean?
- Why does Relationships ask me to confirm some rows?
- What is a "synced view" and why can Baseout only infer it?
- Can I export the diagram?
- I dismissed a relationship by mistake — can I get it back?
