When a Source or Destination loses access, backups stop for every Space that uses it, and Baseout
says so in the connection's status, in a banner on each affected Space, and as an Inbox row. Nothing
is backed up until it is reconnected.

This page will be the shortest route from that message to a working connection.

## Questions this page will answer

- Where do I click to reconnect?
- Do I lose the backups taken before it broke? (No.)
- Are the runs missed while it was broken made up afterwards?
- Why does this keep happening?
- The banner is still there after I reconnected — what now?
